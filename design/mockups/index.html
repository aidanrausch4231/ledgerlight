ledgerlight home: three directions

Same synthetic data in each. The agent has just moved “Dining vs last month” to the top-left and proposes a $400 Dining budget. Open any direction full size for its own light/dark toggle.

1 · Tide Table Open

A harbour almanac: calm serif figures, sea-mist chart paper and depth-blue water, with the agent’s marks printed in chart magenta, the colour real charts use for overprinted notices. Money reads as tides: bills sit in a tide table under a projected “low water” line for Checking.

2 · Gridwork Open

A dense instrument panel on a visible construction grid with lettered columns and numbered rows. Every card has a cell address, so the agent’s moves are literal and auditable: “I9:L12 → A1:D4”, a ghost where the card was and a traced route along the gutters.

3 · Envelope Open

The envelope-budgeting ritual made tactile: coloured envelope stock on a kraft desk, folder-tab navigation, rubber stamps for status and a typed routing slip for bills. The agent works in blue stamp ink, and its proposals arrive as a form that needs your OK.
